This post explores a personal conversation with GPT about overcoming mental fog, slow processing, and performance bottlenecks. The conversation reveals that these issues often stem from internal pressures and over-filtering of thoughts, rather than a lack of intelligence.

The discussion begins with an exploration of the feelings of blankness and mental fog that can arise in conversations, particularly under pressure. **GPT suggests that this is not a sign of low IQ, but rather a result of over-filtering thoughts and getting "stuck"**. This can lead to a pattern of pretending to understand and feeling like one has nothing to contribute.

Some key takeaways from the conversation include:
* **Recognizing that slow processing and over-filtering are habits that can be addressed**, rather than inherent limitations
* **The importance of practicing real-time thinking and reducing self-evaluation**, in order to improve performance in high-pressure situations
* **The need to reframe one's mindset and stop trying to "keep up" with others**, instead focusing on moving conversations forward and speaking freely

The conversation also highlights the importance of context in determining one's ability to articulate thoughts and engage in conversations. **In low-pressure situations, one may be more articulate and confident, while high-pressure situations can lead to freezing and self-doubt**. By recognizing these patterns and working to address the underlying issues, it is possible to overcome mental fog and performance bottlenecks and improve overall communication and confidence.
